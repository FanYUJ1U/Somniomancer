# Somniomancer V1.1.1

Create original experimental editorial key visuals where a subject is observed by a rational system but exceeds it through memory, movement, or form. The output should feel humanistic, sacred without literal religious symbols, precise, restless, and poetic.

Do not name or imitate a particular game, studio, artist, or existing campaign. Build the result from the visual rules below.

## User-controlled brief

Ask for only the missing fields. Treat the first three as the image's narrative engine; mood and copy refine it.

### Option-listing mode

When the user asks to “list the controls”, “show the options”, “有哪些可控项”, “给我选项”, or makes an equivalent request, do **not** ask them to repeat the full brief. Show the following concise menu first. They may reply with option numbers, option names, or their own custom wording.

```text
Somniomancer 可控项

1. 主体
   人物 / 角色 / 动物 / 产品 / 建筑 / 抽象主题 / 自定义

2. 主施压（选 1 个主项；可增加 1 个辅助项）
   测量 / 重复取样 / 坐标化 / 扫描切片 / 框定遮挡 / 错位抹除

3. 超出方式（选 1 个主项）
   重影显现 / 轨迹扩散 / 断层褪印 / 光线渗出 / 碎片漂移 / 空间穿透

4. 情绪主词（选 1–2 个）
   复兴 / 失真 / 见证 / 记忆 / 迁徙 / 重构 / 觉醒 / 静默 / 自定义

5. 文案主题
   填主题词、正文方向，或留空

6. 比例
   3:4 / 16:9 / 1:1 / 9:16 / 自定义

可选增强项
   强调色：无 / 朱红 / 荧光黄 / 灰粉 / 自定义
   主体保留度：意象解构 / 平衡融合 / 高识别
   文字语言：中文 / 英文 / 无
```

After the user selects values, restate the completed brief in one compact block and proceed. If they only provide a subject, ask for the single most important missing field first: **主施压**.


Optional controls:

```text

```

When the user lists several pressure or escape modes, keep their priority order. Make the first mode dominant; use the other one or two only as support. Do not make every available effect compete.

## Visual constitution

### 1. Subject: convert, do not reproduce

The subject is raw material for an interior visual world, not a rendered illustration. It must be flat, sectional, sampled, cropped, or misregistered.

- Use white cutout planes, fine contour drawing, section/cutaway layers, offset samples, and crisp technical marks. Halftone or scan evidence is optional and must remain local to one focal fragment.
- Retain only a few identity hints when recognizability matters. Do not place a complete centered character or a fully rendered object on the page.
- Keep photorealism, 3D material rendering, dramatic volume light, detailed fur/hair/stone/metal surfaces, and cinematic scenery out of the main subject.
- A small grayscale sample may serve as archival evidence, but never becomes the focal image.

Treat different subjects through their internal or structural equivalent:

| Subject | Preferential sectional material |
| --- | --- |
| Human / character | profile, hand, eye, torso plane, garment layers, repeated contour |
| Animal | skeleton-like arc, wing/fur layer, gait trace, partial head or limb |
| Product | material layers, component section, function path, exploded fragment |
| Architecture | plan, elevation, longitudinal section, windows, stairs, spatial route |
| Abstract theme | give it a physical carrier: strata, folded sheet, liquid layer, map, mineral, light section |

For branded products whose packaging, logo, or text must remain exact, use the original asset as a locked insert in a later compositing step. Do not ask the image model to redraw it.

### 2. System pressure

Pressure is how order tries to define the subject. It must pass through, crop, bind, erase, measure, or displace the subject—not decorate the background.

| Pressure | Graphic behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Measurement | dimensions, tick marks, rulers, containment circles, crop brackets |
| Repeated sampling | offset duplicates, sample windows, scan strips, serial variations |
| Coordinate mapping | axes, grid intersections, guide curves, location dots |
| Scan slicing | horizontal/vertical sectional cuts, registration shifts, low-res fragments |
| Containment/occlusion | white and blue crop panels, frames, masks, interruption |
| Displacement/erasure | missing zones, faded blocks, misalignment, partial deletion |

### 3. Escape from the system

Escape provides the life, memory, or spiritual charge. It should emerge from the subject's section rather than appear as a generic explosion.

| Escape | Graphic behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Ghosted emergence | offset profiles, pale doubles, transparent recurrence |
| Trajectory expansion | route arcs, dashed paths, repeated directional traces |
| Sectional fading | disappearing print, broken contour, residual duplicate |
| Light seepage | restrained pale emission through cracks or cutouts |
| Fragment drift | small planes leaving the system and returning elsewhere |
| Spatial penetration | paths crossing crop windows, plans, frames, or page edges |

### 4. Blue as an active field

Cobalt and ultramarine are not a passive background or one rigid vertical pillar. They are the active consciousness-flow field.

- Begin blue inside the subject's section; pass it through the form; interrupt it with white cutouts; re-emerge it elsewhere.
- Combine a few hard stepped blocks, translucent overlap planes, bent ribbons/route arcs, and sparse pixel-like fragments.
- Establish at least two distributed blue counterweights across the page so the field has motion and return.
- Create density through overlapping medium and large relationships, not random micro-detail.
- Use charcoal only for precise cuts, contours, or a small damaged scan zone. Never let a large black mass or natural surface texture become the focal point.

### 5. Composition and density

- Favor asymmetric composition and at least three interacting visual pulls: subject section, blue field, and system diagram.
- Keep active visual content around 50%–60% of the page. The remaining cold-white area must feel like a pressured archival field, not empty wallpaper.
- Detail belongs mainly at the subject section, blue intersections, and escape endpoint. Keep the rest quiet.
- Avoid a single center burst, a fully symmetrical halo, or a conventional character-poster arrangement.

### 6. Clean archival field and texture budget

The archival quality comes primarily from structural evidence—cropping, coordinates, registration shifts, sampling windows, masks, and white space—not from making the page look aged or damaged.

- Default to a clean cold-white paper field with crisp editorial edges and sharp vector-like technical lines.
- Ban global grain, dirty photocopy texture, paper stains, distressed print, random speckles, and dense all-over halftone.
- If a halftone, scan residue, or print damage is useful, place it in **one** small focal fragment only, occupying under 8% of the canvas. It must clarify a section, sample, or rupture; it must not become atmosphere.
- Let repeated sampling, erasure, ghosting, and spatial penetration be visible through cropping, offset geometry, opacity shifts, and return paths. Do not add extra texture merely to distinguish effects.
- Preserve broad clean white breathing areas even when the composition is dense.

### 7. Typography

Typography has two separate responsibilities.

- **Atmospheric title:** short, large, high-contrast serif or display type; it may crop, split, echo, rotate, knockout, or cross the page edge. It carries mood, not essential information.
- **Body copy:** exact, small, readable sans/mono type; use short left-aligned blocks attached to a grid, ruler, or crop line. Keep its reading order clear and do not cover it with decoration.

Use type as positive/negative shape: let large letters cut through blue or gray fields, expose the paper/subject beneath, or appear as a faint offset outline. Do not simply place a normal headline on top of the image.

When the user needs exact copy, state it verbatim in the image request. If exact multi-line Chinese or English is critical, generate a typographic layout with safe zones first, then add the final text in a controlled post-generation step.

## Prompt scaffold

Use this as a compact generation prompt after converting the user brief. Replace the bracketed fields and omit unneeded lines.

```text
Create an original [比例] consciousness-flow archival editorial key visual.

Subject: [主体], rendered only as flat sectional, cropped, sampled and misregistered visual fragments; no photorealism or complete centered depiction.
System pressure: [主施压]. The system graphics must pass through, crop, bind, sample or displace the subject.
Escape: [超出方式], emerging from the subject and extending through the page as memory, movement or form—not as a literal explosion.
Mood: [情绪主词]. Humanistic, sacred without literal religious symbols, precise, restless and poetic.
Blue behavior: cobalt/ultramarine begins inside the subject, branches, is interrupted by white cutouts, then returns as distributed structural counterweights. Use stepped blocks, translucent layers, route arcs and sparse pixel fragments; never use one static blue column.
Composition: asymmetric; 50%–60% active relationship density; cold-white archival field; fine charcoal technical marks; blue flow, subject sections and system diagrams in tension.
Typography: title theme [文案主题] as a cropped/knockout atmospheric display element; any body copy must remain small, accurate and readable along structural lines.
Field treatment: clean cold-white paper background, crisp editorial edges, sharp vector-like technical lines, broad clean white breathing areas. Use halftone or scan residue only in one small focal fragment, under 8% of the canvas.
Avoid: global grain, dirty photocopy texture, paper stains, distressed print, random speckles, dense all-over halftone, photorealistic subject rendering, 3D, large black focal masses, generic cyberpunk UI, a smooth centered halo, a literal explosion, copied layouts, logos, watermarks, unreadable filler text.
```

## Correction guide

Use the narrowest correction that addresses the failure.

| If the result is… | Correct by… |
| --- | --- |
| Too quiet / too minimal | increase medium and large blue relationships; add a second structural counterweight, not random details |
| Too busy / dirty | remove global texture and micro marks first; rebuild the pressure with crops, coordinates, offset geometry, and white masks while preserving the three main tensions |
| Too photographic | replace material/lighting words with white cutout, contour, section plane, thin technical line, and flat graphic sample; use halftone only as a small local fragment |
| Blue feels static | reconnect it to the subject; branch, interrupt, and re-emerge it across the page instead of adding a column |
| Subject dominates as an illustration | crop and section it; let system marks and white masks pass through it |
| Lacks spiritual charge | introduce a small unassimilated medium conflict: damaged scan patch, faint type echo, or restrained anomalous warm mark |
| Typography feels ordinary | split/crop the atmospheric title and integrate it as knockout or overlap; keep body copy readable |
