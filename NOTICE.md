# Notice

## Origin and independence

Somniomancer began as a personal effort to translate an admired feeling of experimental game/editorial art direction into a reusable visual workflow. It distills broad visual ideas—sectional subjects, systems of measurement, displacement, typographic tension, and controlled colour fields—into original operational rules.

It does **not** reproduce, claim ownership of, or grant rights to the art, characters, names, logos, typography, layouts, or other protected material of any game, studio, artist, campaign, or rights holder.
